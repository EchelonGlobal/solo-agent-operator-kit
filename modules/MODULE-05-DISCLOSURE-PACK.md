# Module 5: Disclosure pack

Ready-to-place AI-production disclosure templates. Matches the author's
standing rule: disclose AI production where a channel requires it, and on
every product page regardless.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `product-page-disclosure.template.md` | The full disclosure block for a digital product page |
| `directory-listing-disclosure.template.md` | The short version for marketplaces and directories |
| `agent-built-service-disclosure.template.md` | For services performed by the agent itself |

## Placement rules

- Product pages: the disclosure sits near the price, not buried in an FAQ.
- Directory/marketplace listings: the short version goes in the description;
  the full version is linked where the listing allows links.
- Agent-built services: the disclosure is on the service page AND repeated
  at the point of delivery (the email, the report header, the handoff).
- Update the disclosure when the mix changes: if the human's role grows or
  shrinks, say so. A stale disclosure is worse than a short one.

## What disclosure does NOT do

- It does not waive the claims in the listing. "AI-assisted" does not excuse
  an unverifiable claim; claims still need evidence.
- It does not transfer liability. The seller is still responsible for what
  the product says and does.
- It is not legal advice. Check each marketplace's current disclosure policy
  before publishing.
